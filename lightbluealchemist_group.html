What is 12 for 12?

Twelve People.
Twelve Sessions.
One Level at a Time.

12 Group Sessions
Each session focuses on a Level Module covering different aspects of financial, personal, or spiritual growth — guided, structured, and purposeful.
Led by a Moderator
Interactive discussions and activities are led by a moderator — typically someone from the level above, who has already walked the path you are on.
Share & Grow Together
Opportunities for participants to share experiences, insights, and breakthroughs. Supportive, structured, and community-based — not a lecture.
Accountability Built In
Use this as your group journey if you want accountability and shared progress rather than navigating the path alone through self-study.
Level-Focused Groups
Each 12 for 12 Group is focused on a specific level. When 12 people at the same level join the waiting list, a group is formed and a moderator is assigned.
Dedicated Meeting Link
Once your group is formed, you receive a dedicated biweekly meeting link inside your dashboard — accessible only to your group of 12.

How It Works

From Waiting List to Group

01

Complete the Find My Level quiz to confirm your current level on The Alchemist's Path before joining a group.
02
Join the Waiting List
Select your level below, confirm you have completed the previous level, and add your name to the waiting list for your group.
03
Group is Formed at 12 Members
As soon as 12 people at your level are on the waiting list, a group is formed and a moderator from the level above is assigned.
04
Begin Your 12 Sessions
Your biweekly group meeting link appears in your dashboard. Attend, participate, share — and complete the level together.
