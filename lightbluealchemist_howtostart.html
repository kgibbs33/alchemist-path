The Motivation to Start

Can you visualize what it would look like to never have to worry about money ever again? Bills are always paid, you never have to think which side of the menu to choose from, and more importantly, you have a healthy way of dealing with money so that it flows to you and expands rather than disappearing before the end of the month.

Alchemist Principle

An Alchemist believes that all effort has side effects. The effort you give to your financial situation will have other benefits in other areas of your life, which sometimes you recognize only later.
